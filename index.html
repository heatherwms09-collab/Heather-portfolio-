About — How the models are made

Heather Williams builds the design infrastructure behind products that have to perform under real-world constraints: aerospace components, healthcare devices, and automotive systems. As a Mechanical Design Engineer and CAD Specialist, she translates engineering requirements into precise parametric models and validated assemblies that manufacturing teams can execute.

Her path runs through nuclear and aerospace research at Oak Ridge, multi-sector consulting across automotive, healthcare, and technology, and AI model evaluation — giving her a design toolkit shaped by production realities, not just theory.

She holds a Master's and Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, both from the University of Tennessee, Knoxville's Tickle College of Engineering.
